# Project framing

## One sponsor and one business question
Theme: **AI**. Proposed sponsor: a **DINUM project team planning a pilot of AI coding assistants**. This is an educational case, not an actual commission or partnership.

**Which uses, tools, verification practices and training needs should DINUM prioritise for an initial AI coding-assistant pilot?**

The decision is the scope of a pilot: who to support, which tools and tasks to examine, and which checks or training to consider. The project does not study HR retention or claim to establish AI productivity benefits.

## Shared population
- **2026 only** in the initial version.
- `Country == "France"` and `CodeMain == "I create code as a primary part of my job"`.
- One row per respondent; `ResponseId` is unique within this edition.
- Verified archive: 30,903 respondents, 794 in France, **628 eligible respondents** before interactive filters.
- All industries. These respondents are not necessarily public-sector staff. They provide an exploratory benchmark, not evidence about DINUM's staff or all French developers.
- Include known AI non-users and missing AI answers in the eligible population. Rates for a question use its actual answer denominator.

## Five complementary axes
| Axis | Focused question | Verified fields |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Adoption and profiles | How does reported AI use vary with experience and role? | `AISelect`, `WorkExp`, `Role`, `Employment` |
| 2 — Tools | Which assistants have respondents used and which do they want to try? | `AICodeAgentHaveWorkedWith`, `AICodeAgentWantToUseNext6Months`, `LLMHaveWorkedWith` |
| 3 — Tasks | Where is AI used, expected to expand, or not planned? | `AIWorkCurrentlyUse`, `AIWorkExpectMore`, `AIWorkDoNotPlanToUse` |
| 4 — Verification | What trust conditions and verification practices are reported? | `AITrust`, `AIWorkNext`, `AISentiment` |
| 5 — Support | How do respondents learn AI skills and what matters for adoption? | `LearnAI`, `LearnAIHow`, `AIAdopt` |

## Initial dashboard direction
The initial dashboard reports computed rates and proposes **verification alongside tool access** as a point to investigate for the pilot. This recommendation is provisional. The five owners must deepen the evidence and reconsider the message before submission. The app does not claim to identify the best product or prove an improvement in learning, security or productivity.

## Shared limitations
Voluntary survey, changing response coverage, conditional questions, small filtered samples, and no representative weighting. Multiple selections do not represent different people. A wish to use a tool is not actual use. Associations are not causal effects.
